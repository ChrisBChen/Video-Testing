## Why this topic (and not friction)

The first attempt ("What if friction disappeared?") had a muddy takeaway: lots of things get slippery. This one has a **number, a paradox and a payoff**:

* A clean, visual premise anyone can picture: a hole straight through the planet.
* A hook with an answer you have to see through (42 minutes... to where?).
* A mid-video "huh" (gravity is zero at the center, yet you're doing space-station speed).
* A genuinely counter-intuitive payoff: **any straight tunnel, from anywhere to anywhere, takes the same time**, even one across town.
* A one-line reason you can repeat to a friend: *bigger pull, longer trip, it cancels out.*
* An honest coda (real Earth: ~38 min through the center), which gives viewers something to look up.

### Three hooks considered
1. **"What if you jumped into a hole straight through the center of the Earth?"** Chosen: instant visual, instant stakes, and the question is the title.
2. "Fall through the Earth and you'll arrive in exactly 42 minutes." Strong, but it gives the number away before the escalation.
3. "Dig a tunnel from New York to LA: how long does the ride take?" Relatable, but the center-of-the-Earth fall is the more dramatic opening; the NY–LA tunnel becomes the payoff.
